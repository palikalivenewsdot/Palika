## 3. Numbers — the difference between a claim and evidence

Every `[N]`, `[X]`, `[Y]`, `[YYYY]` in a bullet must become a real figure you can defend at interview:

- team size, contributors, editors
- content volume per week / month and audience size
- number of documents, guides or SOPs maintained
- number of training sessions, workshops or clinics delivered
- adoption %, cycle-time reduction, correction reduction, fall in support queries
- archive size (years and article count)
- project years

**Rule:** if you cannot source a number, rewrite the bullet without one ("…coordinating a team of editors, journalists
and freelance contributors…") or drop the bullet. Never estimate upward, never invent.
