## 4. Context that should shape your wording

- **HR first, then Finance and Procurement.** The role starts inside HR and expands. If you have any HR-adjacent work — recruitment advertising, staff communications, training coordination, rosters, freelance contracts, onboarding — surface it explicitly.
- **EMBL-EBI-specific change, aligned to EMBL-wide practice.** Emphasise experience of working to group-wide standards/house style across multiple teams or titles.
- **Part of a team, not leading the programme.** You work *alongside* the Change & Engagement Manager, Product Manager and Programme Managers, and *support* the Change Manager. Keep verbs collaborative: coordinate, support, maintain, gather, facilitate, act as — not "I led the enterprise transformation".
- **Grade 4, 3-year project contract.** Show you are comfortable with a defined project lifecycle, delivery to deadlines, and working in an international, scientific, multi-site environment.
- **DORA signatory.** EMBL applies DORA principles to recruitment: do **not** add a photo, date of birth, marital status, nationality or religion to the CV. Keep it factual and evidence-based.
